3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by [hosting provider, as stated in your Developer Akademie hosting details]. When you open a page, the web server automatically records technical data in log files: your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested file, the browser and operating system, and the referring page.

This data is needed to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating the website). The log files are kept by the hosting provider only as long as necessary and are not combined with other data. I have no access to the identity behind an IP address.

4. Local storage in your browser

When you switch the sound on or off, the game saves this choice under the key muted in your browser's local storage, so it is remembered on your next visit. This value is not sent to a server and contains no personal data. Because it is strictly necessary for the function you requested, no consent is required.

You can delete it at any time with the button below or through your browser settings.

5. Fonts and external content

The font, images and sounds are loaded from the same server. No data is sent to Google Fonts, content delivery networks or other third parties.

6. Sharing of data

Personal data is not sold or passed on to third parties. The hosting provider processes server log data on my behalf under a data processing agreement.

To exercise these rights, write to the email address above. If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Austrian data protection authority: Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, www.dsb.gv.at.

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal data from children or anyone else, apart from the server log data described above.
